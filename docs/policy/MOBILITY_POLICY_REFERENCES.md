## United Kingdom

Official sources:
- GOV.UK — Work in the UK as a researcher or academic leader (Global Talent visa): https://www.gov.uk/global-talent-researcher-academic
- GOV.UK — Individual fellowship route: https://www.gov.uk/global-talent-researcher-academic/individual-fellowship
- GOV.UK — UKRI endorsed funders: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ukri-endorsement-endorsed-funders-global-talent-visa

Frozen engineering implication:
- A researcher may have a Global Talent route independent of employer sponsorship.
- Current or recent fellowship evidence is mobility-positive, but exact eligibility must be verified at decision time.
- Therefore “no sponsorship” is not an automatic UK skip.
